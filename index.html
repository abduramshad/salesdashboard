BTD = target − achievement. DRR = BTD ÷ balance days (the report date counts as a balance day). CRR = achievement ÷ days elapsed. Trend = CRR × total days. Trend % = trend ÷ target. ≈ marks a monthly target estimated from the quarter target (days-weighted) because the Target sheet has no monthly figure.
